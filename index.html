About the project

Growing a greener tomorrow

The Plantation Community Service Project focuses on the importance of trees and environmental conservation. Through planting and caring for saplings, students learn how individual and community action can improve green cover, support biodiversity and protect the environment. The project encourages responsible plant care beyond the plantation day.

🌳

Why plantation?

Trees absorb carbon dioxide, release oxygen, provide shade and support birds and other living organisms.

💧

Care for saplings

Planting is only the beginning. Regular watering, mulching and protection help young plants survive.

🤝

Community participation

Encouraging neighbours and students to plant and protect trees builds shared environmental responsibility.

Project activities

From preparation to plant care

Site selection

Identify a suitable plantation area with enough sunlight, space and access to water.

Choose saplings

Select healthy, locally suitable tree saplings based on soil, climate and available space.

Planting

Prepare pits, place saplings carefully, refill with soil and provide initial watering.

Maintenance and awareness

Water and monitor saplings, protect them from damage and share the importance of tree care.
